---
name: verify-claims
description: Check every cited claim in a draft against its evidence.
---

1. For each sentence with a citation, open the cited note and its source page.
2. Quote the exact words that support it.
3. Verdict: VERIFIED, OVERCLAIM, QUOTE DRIFT, WRONG PAGE or UNSUPPORTED, with a one-line reason.
4. Never edit the draft. Write the report to `my-work/research/verification.md`.
