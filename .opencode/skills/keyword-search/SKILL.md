---
name: keyword-search
description: Search my own texts for a question, expanding the keywords, and rank the pages.
---

1. Expand the question into terms: English, traditional and simplified Chinese, variant spellings and names.
2. Search every text in `my-work/sources/processed/`.
3. List each page with at least two terms: source, page, terms found, a short context. Most terms first.
4. Write the list to `my-work/research/notes/hits.md`. It is a list to read, not an answer.
5. Note that OCR errors can hide words; read what matters on the page image.
