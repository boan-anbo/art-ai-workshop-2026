1. One image per call.
2. Visible: people, objects, writing, position.
3. Inferred: date, place, purpose, each marked as a guess with its reason.
4. Do not assume the researcher's hypothesis; "none" is an acceptable answer.
5. Save to `my-work/sources/processed/<image name>.md`.
