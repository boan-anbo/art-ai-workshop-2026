Check every rule in:
- Purdue OWL, Chicago 18th edition:
  https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/research_and_citation/chicago_manual_18th_edition/cmos_formatting_and_style_guide/chicago_manual_of_style_18th_edition.html
- my own copy of the manual, if I put one in my-work/sources/raw/

In short:
- footnotes, not author–date
- first note in full; later notes shortened (author, short title, page)
- bibliography at the end, by the author's surname
- Chinese titles in characters, with pinyin
- change only the format, never my wording

When a rule is unclear, look it up in the sources above; do not guess.
