# When and where Lingnan began — five processed sources compared

Drafted 2026-11-06 from the five files in `my-work/sources/processed/`. Locators are
those recorded in each file's own header. Read states differ and are flagged below.
This supersedes `summary-beginnings-v1.md`, which was written before
`corbett-1963-p15.txt` was in the folder and therefore concluded that 1888 was
unsupported.

## Summary

The five files do not give one beginning. The earliest and most specific is
Corbett's: Happer returned to Canton about 1 January 1888, rented a disused
Presbyterian Mission building in Fati (Flower Land) on the south side of the Pearl
River, examined eighty candidates, admitted thirty, and began classes on 28 March
1888 (`corbett-1963-p15.txt`). The College's own 1908 account instead dates its
opening to 3 June 1899 "with its new organization", with six students, names no
place, and treats Macao only as a refuge reached in June 1900
(`ccc-1908-p12.txt`). The 1954 memoir also says 1899 but places the beginning in
Macao, in rented premises, hedged with 大抵 (`memoir-p1-quote.txt`). Corbett's later
page corroborates the Macao move but dates it to early July 1900
(`corbett-1963-p31.txt`). The 2026 Google AI Overview repeats 1888 in Guangzhou and
the 1900–1904 Macao episode (`google-ai-overview.txt`), but it is a summary of
unstated web pages, not evidence in its own right.

## Disagreements

- **⚑ Founding date — 1888 vs 1899.** `corbett-1963-p15.txt` (28 March 1888) and
  `google-ai-overview.txt` (1888) against `ccc-1908-p12.txt` (3 June 1899) and
  `memoir-p1-quote.txt` (1899). The 1888 date now rests on Corbett, not only on the
  AI summary; the 1899 date rests on two sources, one of them the institution itself.
- **⚑ Founding place — Canton/Guangzhou vs Macao.** `corbett-1963-p15.txt` puts it
  precisely at Fati, on the south side of the Pearl River in Canton;
  `google-ai-overview.txt` says Guangzhou; `memoir-p1-quote.txt` says Macao.
  `ccc-1908-p12.txt` is silent on the place, so it neither confirms nor refutes
  either side.
- **⚑ Macao as origin or as refuge.** `memoir-p1-quote.txt` has the school founded
  in Macao in 1899, while `ccc-1908-p12.txt` (June 1900) and `corbett-1963-p31.txt`
  (early July 1900) both record a *move* to Macao. If it was already there from
  1899, both moves need explaining — or the memoir has dated the move to the
  founding year. Unresolved on these files.
- **⚑ Month of the Macao move — June vs early July 1900.** `ccc-1908-p12.txt`:
  "In June of 1900". `corbett-1963-p31.txt`: "Early in July". A one-month gap; the
  Corbett page breaks off mid-sentence, so its surrounding context is not yet read.
- **≈ Agreement on 1904 and on the return.** `ccc-1908-p12.txt` (removed to "its
  present location" in 1904), `google-ai-overview.txt` (returned to Guangzhou in
  1904) and, by implication, the memoir's fifty-year frame. Only the AI summary
  names Guangzhou as that destination.

## Reading the disagreement

*The following is an AI-generated hypothesis, offered as a frame to test, not as a
finding of any source.* The two dates may not be rivals: 1888 fits Happer starting
a college in rented Canton premises, and 1899 fits the same institution being
"newly organized" and reopening — the phrase "With its new organization" in
`ccc-1908-p12.txt` is doing real work, and the fall from thirty admitted in 1888 to
six students in 1899 suggests a relaunch rather than a first intake. On that
reading, the memoir's Macao founding is best explained as the 1900 move
back-dated into 1899, which is exactly the kind of slip a fifty-year-old memory
makes. Testing it requires the pages before `ccc-1908-p12.txt`'s excerpt and
Corbett pp. 15–31 in full.

## Caveats

All five are short page-level excerpts, not full texts. `corbett-1963-p15.txt` is
unchecked against its page image and carries the only explicit day-and-place
founding claim here; `corbett-1963-p31.txt` breaks off mid-sentence;
`ccc-1908-p12.txt` begins mid-account ("With its new organization"), so the
preceding page may well supply the place now missing. `google-ai-overview.txt` is
a screenshot with no visible citations and should be treated as a pointer to
sources rather than as one.
