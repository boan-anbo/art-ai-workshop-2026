## Question

**Where did Lingnan begin, and what did it teach, 1888–1904?**

Two answerable units sit inside that span, and they rest on different evidence:

1. **Origin.** 1888 in Canton (Happer) or 1899, possibly in Macao (the "new
   organization")? The sources disagree, and the disagreement is itself a finding.
2. **Curriculum.** What was actually taught, and in what language? The evidence is
   strong for 1899–1904 and thin for 1888–1899.

*Researcher's framing, not a source claim:* "1888–1904" is a span I have imposed; the
sources suggest it covers two institutions rather than one continuous school.

## What the evidence currently supports

- **A Canton beginning in 1888 is supported, but by a single source** — Corbett p. 15,
  precise as to place, numbers and day, though not yet checked against the scan.
- **A second, reorganized opening in 1899 is attested by the institution itself**
  (1908 pamphlet, p. 9) and by a pupil's memoir, though the memoir places it in Macao.
- **The Macao move is agreed; its date is not** — June 1900 (pamphlet) vs early July
  1900 (Corbett).
- **Teaching was English-medium from the start**, with Chinese language and classics
  alongside Bible and worship; the 1902 list adds mathematics and sciences.
- **The 1888–99 curriculum is not documented** in anything read so far. Only the
  English-first policy and Chinese classics are known for that phase.

## Open concerns

- **Two phases, not one.** The split between Happer's college (1888–98/99) and Wisner's
  reorganisation (1899–1904) is an *AI-generated hypothesis* in
  `notes/early-lingnan-curriculum-evidence.md`; it organises the evidence but is not
  stated by any source.
- **Institutional interest.** The 1908 pamphlet is the College's own account and has a
  stake in the dates it gives; the 1954 memoir is a fifty-year-old recollection with
  faulty OCR behind it.
- **Risk of a smooth narrative.** The disagreements (1888 vs 1899; June vs July 1900;
  Canton vs Macao) should stay visible in the draft rather than be reconciled away.
- **Copyright/access** for archive images must be checked before any are reproduced.
