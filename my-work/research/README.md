# Research (`my-work/research/`)

Put your **research notes, method definitions, comparison tables, and reflections** here.

Follow the two core workshop principles across all notes:
1. **Whose idea is this?** Explicitly distinguish your own interpretation, AI suggestions, and others' scholarship.
2. **Where did this information come from?** Keep the file + page/image region locator attached to every claim.

Suggested files:
- `notes/` — source notes, project decision notes, and cross-project conceptual notes
- `design/` — research questions, side-by-side image comparison notes, or `Evidenced / Inferred / Invented` tables for 3D & virtual heritage scenes
- `outputs/` — checked comparison summaries and presentation notes
