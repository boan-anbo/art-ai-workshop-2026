# Passages

P1 · Analects 9.17
子在川上曰：「逝者如斯夫！不舍晝夜。」

P2 · Plato, Cratylus 402a (on Heraclitus)
Heraclitus says somewhere that all things pass and nothing stays, and comparing existing things to the flow of a river, he says you could not step twice into the same river.

P3 · Ecclesiastes 1:4 (KJV)
One generation passeth away, and another generation cometh: but the earth abideth for ever.

P4 · Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 4.43
Time is a sort of river of passing events, and strong is its current; no sooner is a thing brought to sight than it is swept by and another takes its place.

P5 · 蘇軾《赤壁賦》
逝者如斯，而未嘗往也；盈虛者如彼，而卒莫消長也。

P6 · 敖士洲《五十年前之嶺南》p. 3
星期日……下午則分若干組由各教師分別率領前往各公園、海濱或僻靜之處遊行。
