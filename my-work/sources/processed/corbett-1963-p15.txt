About January 1, 1888 Dr. Happer returned to Canton with his wife, intent on starting the college of his dreams. For temporary quarters he rented from the Presbyterian Mission the building in which he had previously conducted the Training School. It stood in a district called Fati (Flower Land) on the south side of the Pearl River, and had been unused for three years. ... Eighty young men appeared for the entrance examinations, and thirty of the most promising were admitted. ... Classes began on March 28, 1888.
