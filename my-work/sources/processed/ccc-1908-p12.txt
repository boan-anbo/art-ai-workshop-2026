With its new organization the College opened June 3, 1899, with six students. In two weeks the number had doubled, and by November 6th the secretary had registered twenty-nine boys, twenty of whom were in regular attendance. In June of 1900, on account of the disturbed condition of China, the school was moved to Macao, a Portuguese settlement near Hongkong, where it remained until removed to its present location in 1904.
