# AI Tools Workshop for Art Research 2026

Start here: [Session 1, what to do before Thursday, 6 November](session-01/instructions.md)

Workspace for a two-session hands-on workshop, November 2026. You will fork this repository and work in your own copy.

All instructions, materials, and updates for the workshop live in this repository. If something changes, it changes here first.

## The two sessions

1. **Vibe Coding for Art Research** — Thursday 6 November, 2:30–4:30 PM. Set up an AI coding agent on your own laptop and go from a research question to a small working tool: organize your materials, extract text, generate descriptive text for images, and build a small searchable catalog of your own sources.
2. **Vibe Coding for Art Research Presentation** — Thursday 13 November, 2:30–4:30 PM. The presentation side, from capture to publication: 3D capture and Gaussian splatting, IIIF and Mirador, image and video generation, interactive maps, Three.js, web AR, scrollytelling, and a virtual exhibition page assembling your own materials.

You leave with a working setup on your own laptop, a small searchable catalog of your own materials, and web pages that show them, all in a repository you own. Everything keeps working after the workshop ends.

## What is in this repository

- `session-01/` and `session-02/` one folder per session, each with its own `instructions.md`, posted before the session
- `my-work/` where your own work goes, in your fork
- `AGENTS.md` the rules an agent follows inside this workspace

Nothing else needs to be read in advance. Do the session 1 instructions, and we will open the rest together.
